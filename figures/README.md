## A shared motion. A stellar sequence.

File: `figures/motion-and-sequence.png` (2227 × 1305 px); SVG alongside.

**Alt:** Two scatter plots show 866 selected Gaia stars in teal against the gray filtered field. Their proper motions form a compact group near 20 and minus 45 mas/year. The same candidates trace a curved color–magnitude sequence.

**Caption:** Left: two of the three clustering features; parallax is the third. Right: color and magnitude were withheld from the fit, but the query required usable photometry and absolute G reuses parallax. No extinction or parallax-zero-point correction is applied. The field contains 12,520 quality-filtered Gaia DR3 sources, of which 866 pass the candidate rule.

**Credit:** Data: ESA/Gaia/DPAC, Gaia DR3. Comparison: Hunt & Reffert (2024), CDS VizieR J/A+A/686/A42.

## A selection rule you can trace.

File: `figures/candidate-path.png` (2188 × 1322 px); SVG alongside.

**Alt:** A three-step diagram moves from 12,520 filtered Gaia sources to a fitted target component of 1,288, then to 866 candidates after the strength threshold of 0.5. Subsequent checks use held-out features, a comparison catalog and fixed sensitivity experiments.

**Caption:** The model fits parallax and two proper motions after robust scaling. An external rough Pleiades anchor identifies the target component; assignment strength at least 0.5 defines the reported list. This is target-specific selection, and assignment strength is not calibrated physical membership probability.

**Credit:** Data: ESA/Gaia/DPAC, Gaia DR3. Comparison: Hunt & Reffert (2024), CDS VizieR J/A+A/686/A42.

## Overlap is evidence. It is not accuracy.

File: `figures/catalog-overlap.png` (1948 × 1238 px); SVG alongside.

**Alt:** Three bars share an intersection of 781 source IDs. The totals are 866 project candidates, 872 reference sources present in the filtered snapshot, and 1,014 sources in the full comparison subset. The corresponding fractions are 90.2, 89.6 and 77.0 percent.

**Caption:** Comparison subset: Hunt & Reffert (2024), catalog score at least 0.5 and inrt equal to 1, inside the estimated King tidal radius. Both methods use Gaia and related density selection. The 85 candidate-only and 91 eligible-reference-only sources are disagreements, not known errors; 142 reference sources are absent from the retained input.

**Credit:** Data: ESA/Gaia/DPAC, Gaia DR3. Comparison: Hunt & Reffert (2024), CDS VizieR J/A+A/686/A42.

## The threshold changes the answer.

File: `figures/threshold-sensitivity.png` (1793 × 1204 px); SVG alongside.

**Alt:** A line joins counts of 1,099, 866, 582 and 254 candidates at assignment-strength cuts of 0.3, 0.5, 0.7 and 0.9. The original 0.5 setting is highlighted.

**Caption:** All four thresholds apply to the same 1,288-source target component. These are alternative selections, not confidence intervals or membership probabilities. The original 0.5 threshold remains unchanged and no setting was chosen by maximizing catalog agreement.

**Credit:** Data: ESA/Gaia/DPAC, Gaia DR3. Comparison: Hunt & Reffert (2024), CDS VizieR J/A+A/686/A42.
